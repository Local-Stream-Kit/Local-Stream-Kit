The markup and its classes

The look is your CSS: it styles this markup. #stage is the whole screen, and places the bar where the Where setting says, 40 px from the edges.

#stage
  .train …classes
    .head
      .title   the node's Title: text, span.emote > img.emote__image
                 (+ img.emote__overlay), span.text-style.text-style-<name>
      .level   "Level 3"
      .time    "2:41", or "Ended"
    .track
      .fill    as wide as the train has got towards the next level
      .points  "340 / 500" (empty once it has ended)

Classes on .train, each when it applies:

  • .shown while it's up; without it, it's on its way out (or gone), so the look's own transition or animation takes it away
  • .level-up for 2.5 s after a new level, for an animation (added again at each one)
  • .ended once the train has ended, until it goes ("Fade out")
  • .restored shown again after the Browser Source reloaded, so CSS can skip its entrance
  • .at-top .at-middle .at-bottom the edge it's nearest, from Position, to slide in from that side
  • .regular .golden_kappa .treasure the kind of train: the node's Class, {kind} unless you change it (any classes you type there come too)

var(--progress) on .train is how far it has got, from 0 to 1 (1 once it has ended), for a look that draws it some other way than .fill's width.

Fonts can come from Google Fonts (@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?…")).
